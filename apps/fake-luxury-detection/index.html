Fake Luxury Detection

A MobileNetV2 CNN that classifies a product photo as Real or Fake. The trained model runs in your browser.

Prototype model, small data. It was trained on only 85 images (51 fake, 34 real), and the project's validation folder contains the same images as training, so its accuracy on new products is unknown. Treat results as a demo of the pipeline, not proof that a product is authentic.

How it works

Photo→Resize 224×224, ÷255→MobileNetV2 (ImageNet, frozen)→GlobalAveragePooling→Dense 128 ReLU→Dropout 0.3→Dense 1 Sigmoid→> 0.5 = Real
