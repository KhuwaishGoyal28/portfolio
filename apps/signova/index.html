Works best with one right hand, palm facing the camera, fingers pointing up, in good light. The rules assume a right hand, as in the original.

A sign is confirmed after it is held for 18 consecutive frames (about 0.6 s). Letters are joined; words and phrases get spaces around them.

What it can recognise

These are the outputs the original Signova rule engine can actually produce. It is a hand-written geometric rule set on MediaPipe hand landmarks, not a trained classifier, and it does not cover the full A–Z alphabet (letters such as E, J, K, L, N, P, Q, T and Z have no rule). Similar shapes can be confused.
