AI Patient Diagnosis

A small PyTorch neural network trained on synthetic EHR records, running live in your browser.

Educational demo, not medical advice. The model was trained on a synthetic dataset whose Diagnosis labels were generated at random (np.random.choice([0, 1]) in the project's data-generation notebook). There is no real signal to learn, so its predictions are close to 50/50 and carry no clinical meaning.
