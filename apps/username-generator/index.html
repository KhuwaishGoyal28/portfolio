How the prediction works: as in the original notebook, fewer than 5 ratings gives a random score between 3 and 5. After more than 5 ratings the model is retrained. The notebook used scikit-learn's RandomForestRegressor on hashed words; this browser port uses a small k-nearest-neighbour regressor on simple features (length, word lengths, number, special character) instead. Word list: a 10,000-word sample of the NLTK words corpus.
